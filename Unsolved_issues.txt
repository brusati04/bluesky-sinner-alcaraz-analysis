This is a list of issues found while working on this project. These issues are not addressed and are ground for future improvement.
Match-related noise:
- Crawling data during an important match introduces excessive news and neutral comments.
- A proposed improvement is to change the crawling dates to a period outside major matches to reduce this noise.

Foreign language text:
- Comments in other languages (e.g., Spanish) pose a challenge for analysis.
- Suggested solutions: translate foreign comments into English or use a multi‑lingual model.

Missing nested comments/replies:
- The current crawl only captures posts and comments that directly mention Sinner or Alcaraz, but not the replies to those comments.
- This misses relevant conversational context.

Sarcasm:
- Sarcastic comments are difficult to detect and correctly interpret for stance detection.

Heuristic stance formula:
- The stance classification is based on a heuristic (rule‑based) approach, which may not be robust or accurate enough.
- This is acknowledged as a limitation of the current methodology
